## Executive Summary
This audit inspects the Twelve Skies landing page (`src/app/page.tsx`) against the complete Antislop filter specification (`antislop`, `antislop-ui`, `antislop-copywriting`). The page retains remnants of AI-generated aesthetics, including Unicode emoticons/emoji clutter in text, insufficient card transparency caused by heavy opaque dark gradients and solid image containers, decorative arrow/pill overuse, and uniform template grids.

---

## Numbered Findings List

### Finding 01 — Raw Unicode Emoticons & Astrological Symbols in Text
- **Rule:** R-16 (Copywriting) / R-20 (Visual Identity)
- **Priority:** HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Violation:** Raw Unicode characters (`♌`, `♐`, `♑`, `♋`, `♊`) are sprinkled directly into headings, badges, and button labels (e.g. `Leo (♌)`, `♑ Capricorn · Node #02`, `<span style={{ color: c.glowColor }}>{c.symbol}</span>`). This reads as casual AI emoji decoration rather than bespoke celestial typography.
- **Remediation:** Remove raw emoji symbols from text. Replace with clean typographical constellation names and use only our custom SVG vector glyphs (`ZodiacGlyph.tsx`) where iconography is genuinely needed.

---

### Finding 02 — Em Dash Character in Text Copy
- **Rule:** R-02 (Copywriting — Hard Gate)
- **Priority:** HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Violation:** Found em dashes (`—`) in body text: *"Twelve Skies is your private refuge in the cosmos—a dedicated place"*, as well as unspaced hyphens in epochs.
- **Remediation:** Remove all em dashes (`—`). Replace with commas, periods, colons, or clean parentheses per R-02.

---

### Finding 03 — Insufficient Card Transparency (Heavy Opaque Fills & Dark Gradients)
- **Rule:** R-10 (Glassmorphism & Surface Craft) / R-01 (Color & Transparency)
- **Priority:** MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)
- **Violation:** The user explicitly requested transparent cards, but cards currently use dark opaque image blocks with `bg-gradient-to-t from-space-black via-space-black/30 to-transparent` and heavy dark container backgrounds (`bg-space-navy/35`). The background starfield is blocked by opaque layers, making the cards feel like heavy dark solid boxes.
- **Remediation:** Redesign card surfaces into genuine crystalline frosted glass: `bg-white/[0.02]` to `bg-space-navy/15`, with clean thin borders (`border-white/10 hover:border-cyan-glow/40`) and backdrop blur. Strip out the solid black gradient covers so the cosmic starfield is visible through the translucent card glass.

---

### Finding 04 — Generic AI Capsule Eyebrow Badges Above Headlines
- **Rule:** R-09 (Badges) / Part 1 Decorative Elements
- **Priority:** MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)
- **Violation:** A capsule pill badge (`inline-flex items-center gap-2.5 px-4 py-1.5 rounded-full border border-cyan-glow/30 bg-cyan-glow/10...`) with a spark icon is parked directly above the H1 and above every section header. This is a signature AI landing page tell.
- **Remediation:** Eliminate the decorative floating pill badges above headlines. Let the typographic hierarchy speak directly, or use a minimal, clean text label without the glowing pill wrapper.

---

### Finding 05 — Decorative Arrow Icons on Every Button and Card Link
- **Rule:** R-08 (Button Arrows)
- **Priority:** MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)
- **Violation:** `ArrowRight` icons are attached to almost every button, card link, and footer item as pure decoration (Hero CTA, 4 feature cards, 12-constellation CTA, and all footer links).
- **Remediation:** Remove decorative arrows from standard buttons and cards. Reserve directional arrows strictly for true paginated controls or navigation anchors where directional guidance is functional.

---

### Finding 06 — Copy-Paste Uniform 2x2 Feature Grid (Monotonous Rhythm)
- **Rule:** R-05 (Layout & Page Structure) / R-14 (Feature Cards)
- **Priority:** LOW (Quality Lock)
- **Violation:** The 4 features (360° Canvas, Mathematical Engine, 4-Point Sparkle Nodes, Split-View Journal) are rendered in a uniform 2x2 grid with identical heights, image placements, tags, and paragraph lengths.
- **Remediation:** Break the uniform 2x2 template. Give the flagship feature (the 360° Stargazing Canvas) a dominant, wide interactive hero showcase, while organizing the supporting features into an asymmetric, complementary layout.

---

### Finding 07 — Generic Lucide Icon Over-Stacking
- **Rule:** R-04 (Icons — Relevance & Overuse)
- **Priority:** MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)
- **Violation:** Lucide icons (`Sparkles`, `Compass`, `Calendar`, `ShieldCheck`, `Layers`, `Lock`, `MapPin`) are attached to almost every badge, header, and card without functional purpose.
- **Remediation:** Strip unnecessary decorative icons. Keep iconography purposeful, subtle, and tied to true user interactions.
