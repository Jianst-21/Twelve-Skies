# Surface Brief: Twelve Skies Landing & Web Experience

- **Target:** `src/app/page.tsx` (and companion routes `src/app/login/page.tsx`, `src/app/sky/page.tsx`)
- **Mode:** Persuade (landing `/`) and Operate (`/login`, `/sky`)
- **Audience:** Private individuals archiving personal photographs chronologically without social feeds or public timelines.
- **Action:** Sign in to unlock the private 3D vault (`/login`) or explore the 3D celestial dome (`/sky`), and test the date-to-node mapping engine live on `/`.

## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** Replace the generic multi-card cosmic SaaS template with a nocturnal observatory where the Neowise deep-space starfield and pointer-reactive bioluminescent star wakes form the living sky, while every photographic preview and mathematical rule sits as a crisp astronomical instrument over the void.
- **OWN-WORLD:** Pure Space Black (`#000000`) and Midnight Navy (`#03045E` / `#04091e`) layered with the Neowise starfield textures (`10-star-mini.jpg`, `2-star-mini.jpg`) and a 60fps cyan-blue bioluminescent wake canvas (`#7CDEE4`, `#38BDF8`), razor-thin cyan hairlines, `Titillium Web` display typography, `Manrope` body copy, `JetBrains Mono` strictly for astronomical coordinates/dates, and dose-capped translucent inspection lenses.
- **STORY:** A visitor immediately experiences the 12 zodiac constellations glowing in deep space, inspects how any calendar date maps deterministically to a star node (`B = floor(D/N)`), previews illustrative archived plates, understands the private-only sanctuary covenant, and steps directly into `/login` or `/sky`.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Minimal edge-to-edge horizon header (`Twelve Skies`, 4 section links, `Sign In`, `Open 3D Sky`), left-aligned architectural display headline (`Disturb the Dark, Find Your Stars`), one concise sentence, primary/secondary buttons, right interactive constellation star map with one tethered frosted memory inspection lens (`Illustrative Preview`), and a bottom 12-zodiac ecliptic meridian strip.
- **FORM:** Bioluminescent Wake Starfield (`reefs-bioluminescent-nocturnal-plankton-wake-stars`, `seed:cd1b5f73e70bdff4`) fused with the user-requested Neowise cosmic starfield background.
- **FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
